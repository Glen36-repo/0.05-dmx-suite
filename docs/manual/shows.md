# Shows and setlists

A show has sets (e.g. LOW / MID / HIGH). A set either plays scenes on a fixed timer, in order or at random, or generates a fresh scene every step from a style (colours, energy, patterns, movement).

A setlist is the night's song order; each song picks a show and set, or a scene. A pedal steps to the next song.
